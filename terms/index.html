Terms of Use

Effective November 2, 2026. These terms apply to your use of Arch.

Using Arch

Arch records archery practice data you enter and creates summary cards from your selected target photos. Scores are entered manually. Image summaries display those recorded results and do not measure the target or verify shooting accuracy. Follow the rules of your range and responsible archery practices. The checklist records your confirmations and cannot verify physical conditions.

Your content

Use photos you own or are permitted to use. You retain responsibility for the scores, notes and images you add and for images you choose to share. Arch stores this content on your device without requiring an account.

Purchases and exports

Arch is free to download. The first successfully saved summary card is free. Each later new card export uses 1 Arch Seal after the card is successfully saved to Photos. Previews are free. Sharing the same saved card again does not use another seal. Optional consumable packs are available in Your Arch > Get Export Seals and when an export has insufficient balance. The purchase page shows each pack quantity and price. Apple processes payment and displays the applicable price before purchase approval. Purchases are not subscriptions.

Purchase records

Completed consumable purchases have no restore function. Arch processes unfinished transactions and records delivered purchases to prevent duplicate delivery. Clear Range History preserves purchase records, seal balance and first-export usage. Uninstalling the app or losing its local data may remove remaining seals and records. Refund requests are handled through Apple under its applicable policies.

Permissions and availability

Camera and Photos permissions are needed for the related capture, selection and saving actions. The first home appearance also requests notification permission for alerts, badges and sounds. You may change permissions in Settings. Saving and sharing depend on device storage, system permissions and available system services. Store availability and purchase prices depend on the App Store.

Deleting records

Delete a range day or use Clear Range History to remove practice records and associated photos from Arch. Exported images in Photos are separate copies and remain until you delete them in Photos.
