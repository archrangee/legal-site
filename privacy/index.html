Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. This policy describes how Arch handles information when you use the app.

Information on your device

Arch stores range settings, manually entered arrow scores, miss reasons, checklist states, range history, selected target photos and generated summary images on your device. It also stores your Arch Seal balance, first-export usage, purchase transaction identifiers and export completion records to manage purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. Arch does not require an account and does not operate a backend for uploading these records.

Device information

Arch uses the device camera and Photos services only for features you choose. The app does not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts or a device profile for advertising. Local app preferences and purchase state remain on the device. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Camera and photo permissions

Camera permission allows you to take a still target photo. Photos permission allows you to select a target image and save a completed summary card. Arch uses selected images to create your local summary. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. If permission is denied, the relevant capture, import or save action cannot proceed. Arch does not record video or audio and does not request microphone permission. On the first home appearance, Arch requests notification permission for alerts, badges and sounds. You can allow or deny notifications and change this choice in iOS Settings.

Third-party services and sharing

Apple processes purchases through the App Store and provides the camera and Photos system services. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy; Arch does not receive your payment card details. Opening remote policy pages creates a connection to the hosting service, which may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address. When you choose Share Card, the destination you select handles the shared image under its own policies. Arch does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

Retention and deletion

Range days and associated app photos remain until you delete them or clear range history in Your Arch. Clearing history preserves seal balance, purchase transaction records and first-export usage. Images saved to Photos must be deleted separately in Photos. Removing the app removes its local storage; copies in Photos or device backups may remain under your Apple settings.
